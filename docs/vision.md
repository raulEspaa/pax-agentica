# Vision

**The game.** A strategy game ruled by AI: every throne of Europe in 1500 is held by an AI ruler.

**What an AI ruler should do that the greedy bot cannot.** Be a better strategist: think in moves,
not just in the next good action.

**What makes one ruler better than another.** Two things, and both count:

- **It wins:** it conquers and comes out ahead.
- **It is interesting to watch:** it makes moves worth seeing, not merely efficient ones.

**Still to be decided by Raúl.** What is deliberately left out of the first AI ruler, and what he
wants to be able to do when this is finished.
